Privacy Policy

Effective 29 June 2026  ·  Last updated 29 June 2026

Accrue is a personal investment portfolio tracker. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the control you have over it. We've kept it plain and specific — no boilerplate that doesn't apply to us.

01 — Who we are

About Accrue

Accrue ("Accrue", "we", "us") is an application that lets you track your own investments — stocks, crypto, ETFs and commodities, alongside cash accounts, investment properties, dividends and price alerts. You enter and own your data; the app helps you make sense of it.

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at contact@tryaccrue.app.

02 — What we collect

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the app for you:

  • Account details. Your email address, used to create and secure your account. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and stored in encrypted form — we never see or store it in plain text.
  • Portfolio data you enter. The holdings on your watchlist, your buy/sell/dividend trade history, cash account balances, investment property values, price alerts, and your preferences (theme, display currency, benchmark, and onboarding status).

To show live prices and charts, the app sends the ticker symbols you're tracking to third-party market-data providers. These requests don't include your name, email, or account identity.

03 — What we don't do

What we never collect or do

  • No bank or brokerage logins. You enter your trades manually. Accrue never asks for, connects to, or stores your bank or brokerage account credentials.
  • No analytics or tracking. Accrue contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We don't profile you or follow you across other apps and websites.
  • No selling or sharing. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with data brokers or advertisers. Ever.
  • No location, contacts, or device tracking. The app doesn't access your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for tracking.
04 — How we use it

How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: storing your portfolio and showing your performance, insights, and dividend forecasts.
  • To sync your data across your devices when you're signed in.
  • To deliver the price alerts you choose to set up.
  • To secure your account and keep the service working reliably.
05 — Storage & security

How your data is stored and protected

Your account and portfolio data are stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, and the app is hosted via Vercel. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access is protected by per-account security rules so that your records are only accessible to you when signed in.

No method of transmission or storage is ever completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information and work only with providers who maintain strong security practices.

06 — Third parties

Third-party services we rely on

Accrue depends on a small number of infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase — secure database storage and account authentication.
  • Vercel — application hosting and delivery.
  • Market-data providers — live and historical prices for the symbols you track.
  • Apple — app distribution through the App Store.

These providers process data only to perform their function for Accrue. We don't share your information with anyone for marketing or advertising.

07 — Your control

Your rights and choices

  • Access & correction. Your data is visible and editable inside the app at any time.
  • Deletion. You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the app. Once deleted, your data is removed from our systems and cannot be recovered.
  • Questions or requests. Email us at contact@tryaccrue.app and we'll help.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, or the Australian Privacy Act. We honour those rights for all users.

08 — Retention

How long we keep your data

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data and portfolio records are removed from our active systems.

09 — Children

Children's privacy

Accrue is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10 — Changes

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Accrue evolves. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be communicated within the app where appropriate.

11 — Contact

Get in touch

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Reach us at contact@tryaccrue.app.
